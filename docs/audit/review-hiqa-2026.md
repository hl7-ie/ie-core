# Independent review: HIQA 2026 alignment (branch `feat/hiqa-2026-alignment-c8ff80`)

## Summary

The branch is a large, well-documented realignment of IE Core to the HIQA draft EP and PS standards. The mapping is honest: gaps are marked Partial or Gap rather than hidden. Placeholders are mostly labelled, and CI has been properly hardened. I spot-checked 24 EP and 22 PS Mandatory elements against the FSH, the generated StructureDefinitions and the traceability matrix. Most claims hold. The main problems are in the safety invariants:

- **Controlled drugs:** the rules can be bypassed, and one of them rejects lawful instalment prescriptions.
- **Allergy statement:** it is not tied to the patient on the prescription.
- **Paediatric age:** the under-12 rule misses year-only birth dates.
- **Invented content:** a number of authoritative-looking URIs, OIDs and refset IDs are still published, which breaks the project's own "never invent" rule.
- **Documentation:** in several places it claims more than the artefacts deliver.

The read-only checks `check_ep_data_minimisation.py`, `check_page_links.py` and `generate_traceability.py --check` all pass.

## What looks good

- **Honest mapping.** Mandatory gaps are marked Partial or Gap rather than hidden: EP 2.9.x, 4.9.1; PS 6.3.9, 8.2.1, 15.3.3. The other spot-checked Mandatory elements match the profiles or the parent packages, including EP 1.1.2/1.1.3, 1.2.x, 1.4.1-1.4.3, 3.1-3.5.8, 4.7.2, 6.2-6.8 and PS 5.3.x, 7.3.2, 9.3.4, 19.1.x.
- **Labelled placeholders.** Identifier systems are clearly labelled `PLACEHOLDER` NamingSystems (OI-003). The MDA schedule, supply legal status and NMPC code systems are experimental placeholders. The PPSN slice is deliberately not MustSupport.
- **SNOMED CT Irish Edition.** The ValueSets use `http://snomed.info/sct` with version `http://snomed.info/sct/1601000220105`.
- **EP data minimisation.** Ethnicity, mother's maiden name, nationality, religion, marital status and contact are 0..0, with GDPR rationale, and a guard script enforces it.
- **Negative BDD tests.** Every EP invariant has a failing-case test.
- **Scenarios S1–S6 are internally consistent:**
  - S4: 28 tablets = 2 × 14, with a 14-day validity period.
  - S3: 12 inhalers = 2 × (1 + 5 repeats).
  - S2: age 5 matches the date of birth.
  - S5: the decline matches the patient's recorded penicillin allergy.
- **CI hardening.** Actions are pinned to commit SHAs, permissions are least-privilege, jar SHA-256 is checked, and the PR-comment job is limited to same-repository PRs.
- **Clinical safety log.** It records its residual risks candidly.

## Not reviewed

- SUSHI and the IG Publisher were not run, because the review was read-only. I relied on the committed `fsh-generated` output and `validation-results.md`.
- The R5 track, the logical models and most PS entry profiles were not reviewed in depth.
- PS scenarios S7 and S8 were not checked beyond their validation status.
- Action commit SHAs were not verified against upstream.
- HIQA wording was checked only for EP 3.5.7–3.5.13, using the local untracked text extract of the PDF.
- Clinical dosing was not assessed beyond checking the examples for internal consistency.
